OPENING CITY MASTER SHOT. From the crown of a rooftop water tower, look down into Gutter City as a storm front rolls west to east: elevated rail lines crossing over drained pools, the old industrial roofs, the market under the overpass, a block of lit windows, and a tiny magenta arcade glow deep below. Foreground, THE CHRONICLER stands centered with his back to camera: hooded heavy coat and scarf, ink-stained fingers near a battered manual typewriter on a crate, three crows on the wet parapet. Distant crews are only tiny silhouettes in their own neighborhoods. One lightning flash reflects across the whole city, then darkness returns. The city is the scale, the Chronicler is the human anchor. Extreme high-angle establishing shot, vertical city canyon, clear foreground / middle ground / background, no collage, one coherent location.
